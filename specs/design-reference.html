1B

Drafting table

Light, quiet, editorial. Panel chrome disappears into hairlines and whitespace; the canvas is white paper. The argument the diagram makes — decisions, revisions, what changed — is treated as first-class content rather than a modal full of sections.

01 / CHROME
No nested cards. One hairline separates each region, panels share the paper, and the only filled surfaces are the things you can act on.
02 / RAIL
Rail carries words, not just glyphs, and ends in the system's own name — switching projects stops living behind a chevron in the title bar.
03 / REVISIONS
The project modal becomes a timeline in the right panel: revisions, checkpoints and runs on one spine, each with its diff in plain language.
04 / NODES
Nodes are typeset plates: name in text, numbers in mono, a single coloured spine for kind. Notes pin to the margin like manuscript annotations.
